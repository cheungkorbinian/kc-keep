# Third-party assets

本仓库**不包含**下列美术资源。请自行获取并置于项目根目录（与 `game/` 同级）后再运行游戏。

## Tiny Swords

- **Asset:** [Tiny Swords](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords) (Free Pack / Update packs / Enemy Pack as used locally)
- **Author:** Pixel Frog
- **Notes:** Follow Pixel Frog’s license and credit requirements when distributing a build that includes their art.

## Not in this repository

The following local folders are intentionally gitignored and must not be committed:

- `Buildings/`, `Units/`, `Terrain/`, `UI Elements/`, `Particle FX/`
- `Tiny Swords (Enemy Pack)/`, `Tiny Swords (Update 010)/`
